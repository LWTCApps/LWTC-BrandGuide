Logo

Three lockups, supplied as SVG vectors and transparent PNGs.

Primary: black wordmark, for white or light groundsSVGPNG
Icon cluster, for avatars and small spacesSVGPNG
Reversed: white wordmark, for dark or photographic groundsSVGPNG

Suggested usage

  • Keep clear space around the logo of at least a quarter of the height of the letter L.
  • Below about 48 px tall, use the icon cluster instead of the full lockup.
  • Use the reversed logo on dark grounds.

Avoid

  • Recoloring, stretching, rotating or outlining the logo.
  • Adding shadows or effects.
  • Placing the black wordmark on a dark ground.

These usage rules are suggestions, not official college guidelines.

Contrast. The four hues do not reach 4.5:1 against white. Use them as large fields and illustration, and set text in the wordmark ink (#231F20). The gold start value is estimated from the lightest sampled pixels.

Typography

The wordmark is an ultra-heavy Clarendon-style slab serif. That is a visual match: the original font file is unconfirmed.

Aa
Headlines: Alfa Slab One

Fallbacks: Clarendon Black, Rockwell, Roboto Slab, Georgia. Loaded from Google Fonts when online.

Aa
Text: Source Sans 3

A quiet sans that lets the slab headlines lead. Weights 400, 600 and 700.

Shape

Every round form is a speech bubble: a circle with one square corner pointing toward the group center.

Spacing and radius
