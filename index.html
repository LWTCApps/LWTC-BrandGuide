Logo

Three lockups, supplied as SVG vectors and transparent PNGs.

Primary: black wordmark, for white or light backgroundsSVGPNG
Icon cluster, for avatars and small spacesSVGPNG
Reversed: white wordmark, for dark or photographic backgroundsSVGPNG

Suggested usage

  • Keep clear space around the logo of at least a quarter of the height of the letter L.
  • Below about 48 px tall, use the icon cluster instead of the full lockup.
  • Use the reversed logo on dark backgrounds.

Avoid

  • Recoloring, stretching, rotating or outlining the logo.
  • Adding shadows or effects.
  • Placing the black wordmark on a dark background.

These usage rules are suggestions, not official college guidelines.

Typography

The brand font is ITC Neue Aachen Pro Bold (NeueAachenPro-Bold), used for the wordmark and headlines. An ultra-heavy Clarendon-style slab serif is the approved alternate.

Aa
Brand font: ITC Neue Aachen Pro Bold

PostScript name NeueAachenPro-Bold. A licensed font: it shows here only on computers where it is installed.

Aa
Alternate: Alfa Slab One

An ultra-heavy Clarendon-style slab serif for when Neue Aachen Pro is not available. Fallbacks: Clarendon Black, Rockwell, Roboto Slab, Georgia. Loaded from Google Fonts when online.

Aa
Text: Source Sans 3

A quiet sans that lets the slab headlines lead. Weights 400, 600 and 700.

Shape

Every round form is a speech bubble: a circle with one square corner pointing toward the group center.

Spacing and radius

Spacing steps: 4, 8, 16, 24 and 48 px. Corner radii: 4, 12 and 48 px (the bubble).
